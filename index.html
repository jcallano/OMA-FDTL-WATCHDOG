1. Regulatory Framework & Scope

This Progressive Web Application (PWA) is engineered for Oman Air flight and cabin crews to audit, track, and simulate the regulatory legality of monthly rosters, historical flight logs, and last-minute schedule modifications (via the What-If Simulator).

All calculation rules and limits strictly implement the requirements of the Oman Air Operations Manual Part A (OM-A), Chapter 7 based on Muscat Main Base (MCT, UTC+4), valid as of August 2026.

3. Maximum Daily FDP & Chocks-On Principle (OM-A 7.1.6.9 - Table A)

A Flight Duty Period (FDP) commences at the designated reporting time and terminates strictly at Chocks-On (in-block) of the final commercial sector. The subsequent 30-minute post-flight debrief period does not extend the FDP, but establishes the exact release timestamp from which mandatory preceding rest commences for the subsequent duty.

4. Standby Duties & Callouts (OM-A 7.1.7)
  • Home Standby (`SBY`): Credited at 25% towards cumulative duty limits (7d, 14d, 28d) per OM-A 7.1.7.8(c).
  • The 6-Hour Callout Rule:
    • Callout within the first 6 hours: Maximum allowable FDP is not reduced, and FDP counts from airport reporting time (OM-A 7.1.7.8.f).
    • Callout after 6 hours: Maximum allowable Table A FDP is reduced minute-by-minute for all standby time exceeding 6 hours (OM-A 7.1.7.8.g).
  • Airport Standby: Counts at 100% duty credit (OM-A 7.1.7.4).
5. eCrew Schedule Importer & Smart Logbook
  • Roster Parsing & Days Off: Automatically detects OFF and COFF days, highlighted in green badges, and resets consecutive duty day counters to zero.
  • Station Local Time to UTC Conversion: Auto-converts station local times in eCrew reports to UTC based on international IATA airport databases (ZRH, BLR, RUH, TAS, TZX, DXB, etc.).
  • Smart Deduplication: Multiple monthly rosters or overlapping logs can be imported safely; old entries are updated without creating duplicate sectors.
  • Interactive Timeline & Desktop Hover Cards: Move the mouse over any duty row to inspect real-time legal margins, preceding rest, and consecutive duty count.
  • Reset & Update Tool: Purges local database and browser cache, forcing an instant live download of the latest code from GitHub Pages.
🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy & Full Offline Functionality

All roster schedules and flight log data are stored strictly within your browser's private local storage (LocalStorage). No flight hours or personal schedules are transmitted to external servers. The app, database, and this manual operate entirely offline during flights and layovers.
